Privacy Policy for Zombie Survival

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Zombie Survival is developed and published by KaPara Games.

Information We Collect

Zombie Survival does not require users to create an account.

The game uses Unity diagnostic services to help identify crashes, troubleshoot technical issues, and improve application stability and performance.

Depending on the diagnostic services operating in the app, the following technical information may be collected:

  • Crash logs: Information about application crashes and errors.
  • Diagnostics: Technical information used to identify and resolve application problems.
  • Device or other identifiers: Device-related identifiers that may be used to associate diagnostic information with a device or application installation.

This information is used for troubleshooting, performance analysis, and improving game reliability.

Advertisements

The current version of Zombie Survival does not display advertisements. If advertisements are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures will be updated accordingly.

In-App Purchases

The current version of Zombie Survival does not offer in-app purchases. If in-app purchases are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Third-Party Services

Zombie Survival uses Unity technologies, including diagnostic services that may process technical information to identify crashes and improve application stability.

The game may also rely on platform services provided by Google Play, Android, or the device operating system.

These services may process information according to their applicable privacy policies.

Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

We aim to protect technical information processed through the game's services. Security measures, including encryption during transmission where supported, depend on the services involved.

Children's Privacy

Zombie Survival does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Technical diagnostic information may be processed when the game is used, as described above.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features, services, or data practices change. Any changes will be published on this page.
